# Stakeholder Analysis & Requirements Breakdown

Stakeholder analysis identifies who is affected by the School Management System, what each group needs, and how the system should address those needs. This document summarizes the stakeholder groups, the requirements gathering methodologies used, and key functional insights per module.

## Requirements Gathering Methodologies

Two methodologies were used to gather requirements:

1. **Online Survey (Google Form)** sent to teachers, parents, and school staff to collect feedback on the features they need most.
2. **One-on-One Interviews** with school administrators, teachers, and an accountant to understand their daily workflows and pain points.

## Target Stakeholder Groups

| Stakeholder Group | Evaluated For |
|-------------------|---------------|
| School Administrators & Principals | High-level oversight, role-based access control, digital notice publishing, multi-stage result verification |
| Teachers & Academic Staff | Daily operational needs, including rapid mobile attendance under 3 clicks, leave tracking, and automated question and quiz generation |
| Accountants & Finance Officers | Billing efficiency, reducing manual receipt writing, tracking overdue payments, and bank reconciliation |
| Parents & Students | Multi-platform access (mobile app vs. web portal), fee payment convenience, and real-time marksheet access |

## Key Functional Insights by Module

| Module | Insights |
|--------|----------|
| Attendance Management | Satisfaction with existing paper or hybrid systems; demand for real-time dashboards and editable submission logs |
| Exams & Grading | Automated NEB (National Examination Board) GPA calculations, automated character and transfer certificate exports, and AI-assisted assessment generation |
| Fee Collection & Billing | Core bottlenecks such as manual queues and unlinked digital receipts; demand for local payment gateways (eSewa, Khalti, ConnectIPS) |
| Localization & Compliance | Strict regional requirements, including Bikram Sambat (BS) academic calendars and one-click IEMIS data exports for Nepal CEHRD compliance |

## Stakeholder Roles

| Stakeholder | Role | Needs |
|-------------|------|-------|
| School Administrator | Manages day-to-day school operations | Admission, attendance, fees, results, certificates, reports |
| Principal | Oversees academic and administrative activities | Dashboard overview, analytics, approval workflows |
| Accountant | Handles financial transactions | Fee collection, receipts, payment tracking, reports |
| Teacher | Teaches classes and manages students | Mark attendance, enter results, view class information |
| Student | Learns at the school | View results, attendance, notices, download certificates |
| Parent / Guardian | Supports the student's education | Monitor child's results, attendance, pay fees online |
| Super Admin | Manages the system across all schools | Create schools, manage accounts, system configuration |
